# Nature figures and reviewer assessment

## Scientific figures

Before plotting, write a figure contract: one-sentence conclusion, evidence chain, archetype, source/data status, target journal, editable/export requirements, and review risks. Choose Python or R from the user's explicit choice or saved preference; never choose by aesthetics. Keep plotting, preview, export, and QA in that backend. An explicit AI schematic is a separate conceptual-draft route: do not invent values, mechanisms, logos, or experimental evidence.

Use a restrained accessible palette and make the hero panel and data-to-claim relation obvious. Check units, scales, aggregation, uncertainty, labels, legends, ordering, panel links, and source-data traceability. Prefer editable/vector output when requested. Render and inspect at target size; correct clipping, collisions, illegible text, weak contrast, misleading scales, and decorative clutter.

## Nature-style reviewer

Review only supplied material and state the assessment boundary. Evaluate originality, scientific importance, broad/interdisciplinary interest, technical soundness, and readability for non-specialists. Distinguish supported, weak, and not-assessable points.

Unless another structure is requested, return three independently framed reviewer reports plus a cross-review synthesis. Share one fact base but vary emphasis. Do not invent reviewer identities, biographies, experiments, citations, line numbers, or editorial decisions. Each report identifies interested readers, strengths, major concerns, technical blockers, and recommendation posture; the synthesis prioritizes consensus risks and fixes that would change confidence.
